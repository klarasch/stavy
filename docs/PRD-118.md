# PRD-118 — Expense approvals (excerpt, mock)

## §2 Submission
Employees submit expenses through a guided flow (details, receipt, review, confirmation).
Acceptance: the submitted expense appears in the employee's list as *Submitted*.

## §3 Approvals
Managers review submitted expenses from a queue; they can approve, reject, or request changes.
Tracked as JIRA-ORB-412.

## §4 Reimbursement
Finance works the approved queue and marks expenses reimbursed; reimbursed is terminal.
